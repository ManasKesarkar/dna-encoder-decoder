Encoding scheme
How the numbers are calculated
  • Strand length: 0.34 nm per base pair (B-form double helix).
  • Mass: about 330 g/mol per nucleotide of single-stranded DNA.
  • Oligos: real systems write many short strands, not one long one. Each here is 200 nt: 20 nt primer + 16 nt address + 144 nt data + 20 nt primer.
  • 25% extra oligos are added for error correction (an assumed, typical overhead).
  • Primers shown are example sequences.
